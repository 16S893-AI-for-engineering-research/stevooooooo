Lab Notes

A little more about where this work lives and what I'm generally interested in.

Where I work

I'm a Master's student in Aeronautics & Astronautics working with Professor Wood out of the MIT Media Lab. It's an environment that blends hands-on fabrication with hard aerospace engineering, which suits how I like to think about problems: build something, break it, learn, repeat.

Research interest

Broadly: making anything in space. That means additive manufacturing, robotics, and autonomous assembly aimed at removing the constraint that everything useful in orbit has to be built on Earth first and launched whole. The spider-bot concept on the project page is the current focus of that interest.

Why the truss animation

The little bot crawling across the top of every page is a nod to the actual concept: a small climbing robot that never needs the whole structure to exist before it starts moving across it. Try clicking it anywhere on this site — it's the same one, three different times.
